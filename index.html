Financial settlement & risk hedging

Fifteen years of certainty

A 15-year contract for difference, settled against the market price

The wholesale price (SMP) moves every hour, every year

Settlement = contract price − SMP, paid in both directions

Net result: the producer earns the contract price, flat, for 15 years

Two parts: a fixed component in KRW and a fuel component

The fuel component follows KRW / USD, reset typically once a year

Shortfall or surplus within ±10–20% rolls into the next year (up to 1–2 years)

Bankable, USD-linked, fifteen-year off-take
